2. Principles

  • The app contains no trackers, no advertising and no analytics.
  • Data is processed only so you can plan trips and share them with your fellow travelers (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • The app works offline: your trips are also stored on your device and synced when you are online.

3. Data we process

Account: email address, display name, language and theme. With “Sign in with Apple” we receive a user identifier from Apple and – if you agree – your name and a (possibly relayed) email address.

Trip content: what you and your fellow travelers enter – e.g. destination and dates, tasks, packing lists, flights, bookings, contacts, notes and uploaded documents (files, photos, scans). Only members of the trip can see it, according to their role. Private notes are visible only to their author.

ID details (“Entry” section, e.g. passport number and date of birth) are stored only on your device (iPhone or iPad), encrypted, protected by Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode and never sent to us or anyone else – not even in backups.

On your device only: deadline reminders (notifications) and events in a separate “Travelplanner” calendar, if you allow it.

4. Processors

Supabase (Supabase Inc., USA) runs the database, sign-in and file storage. Data is hosted in the region West EU / Ireland (eu-west-1), under a data processing agreement including EU standard contractual clauses.

Apple: Sign in with Apple, place search (Apple Maps) when entering destinations and accommodation, and voice input for lists. Speech recognition runs on the device where possible; otherwise the recording is sent to Apple for recognition. Apple's privacy policy applies.

5. Other sources without personal data

Our server downloads exchange rates once a day from a free dataset (fawazahmed0/exchange-api); no data about you is sent. Travel info (plugs, emergency numbers etc.) is built into the app. Links to the German Foreign Office, map apps or photo albums open those websites or apps under their own privacy policies.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept as long as your account exists. Deleted entries are first marked as deleted so all devices remove them. You can delete your account in the app at any time (Settings → Delete account). This permanently deletes your account, your private notes and trips where nobody else has an account. In shared trips, content remains for the other members; your name stays there as a person without an account.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Contact us at the address above. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

8. Security

All transfers are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to trips is enforced on the server by rules per trip and role.
